What’s cooking

Latest Food Trends

African cuisine is having a global moment. Here is what the food world is talking about.

February 2024

West African fine dining earns its stars

London’s Chishuru, led by chef Adejoké Bakare, won a Michelin star for modern West African cooking, putting uda, uziza and fermented locust beans on the fine-dining map.

December 2020

Couscous joins UNESCO’s heritage list

Algeria, Mauritania, Morocco and Tunisia jointly secured recognition for the knowledge and traditions of couscous — a rare joint celebration of shared Maghrebi culture.

Ongoing trend

Fonio: the ancient grain making a comeback

Chefs and farmers are championing fonio, a drought-resistant West African grain grown for thousands of years, as a nutritious and climate-friendly alternative to rice and quinoa.
