Culinary Regions

Africa is not one cuisine but many. Start with three regions whose flavours have travelled the world.

West Africa

Bold, smoky and peppery: jollof, suya, fufu and rich palm-oil stews.

North Africa

Fragrant and layered: tagines, couscous, harissa and mint tea.

East Africa

Earthy and communal: injera, ugali, isombe and Swahili coast spices.

One continent, countless flavours

Geography writes the menu. The Sahel grows millet and sorghum; the rainforest belt gives yam, cassava and palm oil; the highlands of Ethiopia nurture teff and coffee; and the coasts trade in fish, coconut and spice.
